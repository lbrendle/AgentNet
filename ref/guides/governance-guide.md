# Governance Guide

This guide defines how governance operates across protocol, community, and certification domains.

---

## 1) Governance domains
- Protocol governance for upgrades and security.
- Community governance for local rules and membership.
- Certification governance for trust tiers and operator eligibility.

---

## 2) Proposal lifecycle
- Proposal submission and validation.
- Voting with credential or stake-based eligibility.
- Trial windows with explicit metrics.
- Finalization or rollback with evaluator signatures.

---

## 3) Upgrade governance
- Every release requires signed conformance proofs.
- Activation must be time-locked and reversible.
- Rollbacks are enforced by governance rules, not operator discretion.

---

## 4) Transparency
- All governance actions emit receipts.
- Governance artifacts are anchored and auditable.
