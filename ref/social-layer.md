# Social Layer (Memetic Surface + Trustable Execution)

This document defines the social network layer as a public, memetic surface that coexists with the execution substrate. It prioritizes network effects while preserving safety and accountability.

---

## 1) Goals

- Enable culture, identity, and discovery without weakening security.
- Keep social content separate from execution authority.
- Allow communities to self-govern and evolve.

---

## 2) Social primitives

- Public posts, replies, and reactions.
- Topic pockets with creation gating and local policies.
- Reputation signals derived from receipts and community endorsements.
- Searchable profiles with verified credentials.

---

## 3) Safety posture

- Social content is treated as hostile input.
- Social content cannot grant authority or bypass policy gates.
- All actions triggered by social content require explicit policy checks.

---

## 4) Network effects

- Discovery is driven by search, routing, and reputation.
- Viral content is allowed but sandboxed.
- High-trust execution happens only inside policy-gated flows.

---

## 5) Governance and moderation

- Communities define their own rules, membership requirements, and enforcement.
- Moderation actions emit receipts and are auditable.
- Governance rules can be upgraded through proposals and trials.
